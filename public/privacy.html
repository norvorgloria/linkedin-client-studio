Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Draft pending operator review: Confirm the deployed host, its logging practices, retention and support address before representing this notice as final.

Information submitted

The MCP tools process information you choose to provide, such as client expertise, audiences, goals, professional stories, draft posts and performance metrics. Do not submit passwords, tokens or material that you lack authority to process.

How information is used

Submissions are processed to return the requested brief, calendar, draft review or analytics calculation. The application code does not create persistent client records or intentionally log submitted content.

Infrastructure

When hosted, the hosting provider may process IP addresses, request metadata and operational logs for delivery, security and troubleshooting. Confirm the provider, locations and retention practices before launch. ChatGPT and other MCP clients process conversation data under their own policies.

Third-party connections

The service does not access LinkedIn accounts, connect to a customer CRM, or send material to a separate third-party AI API. Infrastructure providers are used to operate the server.

Retention and security

The application is designed to be stateless. Hosting logs may still exist. HTTPS and sensible operational safeguards should be configured before public use. Absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

Your choices and contact

Do not provide information you do not want processed. Contact admin@glorianorvor.com for privacy inquiries. This notice will be updated as the service evolves.
