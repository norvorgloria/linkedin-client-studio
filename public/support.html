Support

For questions about installation, MCP connections, draft quality checks and privacy requests, contact admin@glorianorvor.com.

What to include

Describe what you were attempting, the approximate time and any error message. Please do not email client credentials, access tokens or confidential client materials.

What the plugin cannot do

It cannot access LinkedIn accounts, send messages, publish content or store client records in a shared CRM. Human review and client permission remain essential.

Privacy requests

Email the contact above with the subject “LinkedIn Client Studio — Privacy Request”.
